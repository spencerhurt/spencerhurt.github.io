## Current Research

Great Basin shrublands are rapidly converting non-native annual grasslands, thanks in large part to cheatgrass (_Bromus tectorum_) invasions that fuel a high-intensity, high-frequency fire regime. The sagebrush steppe—one of the most beautiful and expansive wildlands remaining in the United States—<a href="https://www.nature.org/en-us/what-we-do/our-priorities/protect-water-and-land/land-and-water-stories/sagebrush-sea/">is disappearing at an accelerating rate</a>
